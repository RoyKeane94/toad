Why it works
01

Start in under a minute

There's nothing to configure and nothing to migrate. Create one grid, add columns that match how you already think, and go. No onboarding flow, no tutorial.

02

See stages, not just lists

A list is fine for quick notes. A grid shows progress, loose ends, and what's next, all at once. Projects feel understandable rather than scattered.

03

One grid per thing

Keep separate grids for different projects, clients, or areas of life. Open one, close it, keep your attention where it belongs. Most people use three or fewer.

Toad fits the way you already work

Most tools ask you to change how you work. Toad doesn't. It gives you a clearer surface for information you're already keeping somewhere: in spreadsheets, sticky notes, half-finished docs.

Start with one real project. Drop in the columns that make sense to you. You'll know in a few minutes whether it fits, and for most people, it fits immediately.

It's not a system. It's a window into the work you're already doing.
